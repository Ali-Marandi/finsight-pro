# FinSight Pro Privacy Policy

**Last updated: January 2025**

FinSight Pro LLC ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected
- **Usage analytics** (anonymous): Feature usage frequency, session duration, error reports
- **Hardware fingerprint**: Used for license validation (hash only, no personal data)
- **Application logs**: Local only, never transmitted unless you opt in to crash reporting

### User-Provided
- **Email address**: For waitlist, license delivery, and support
- **Financial data**: All financial statements you analyze are processed **locally on your device** and never uploaded to our servers
- **Payment information**: Handled by LemonSqueezy (Merchant of Record) — we never see your credit card details

## Data Storage

| Data Type | Storage Location | Transmission |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|
| Financial statements | Local SQLite (your device) | Never transmitted |
| Analysis results | Local SQLite (your device) | Never transmitted |
| License key | Local (encrypted) | Validated via API call |
| Email | LemonSqueezy / our email service | Encrypted in transit |
| Usage analytics | Anonymous, aggregated | Optional, opt-in only |

## Third-Party Services

- **LemonSqueezy**: Payment processing, tax compliance (MoR)
- **Stripe**: Enterprise invoicing (data processed under Stripe's privacy policy)
- **Sentry** (optional): Crash reporting, only if user opts in

## Data Retention

- Financial data: Stored indefinitely on your local device until you delete it
- License records: Retained for the lifetime of the license + 2 years
- Email addresses: Retained until you request deletion
- Anonymous analytics: Aggregated and retained for product improvement

## Your Rights

- **Access**: Request a copy of your data (email privacy@finsightpro.com)
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your email and account data
- **Opt-out**: Disable analytics in app settings at any time
- **Data portability**: Export all your local data via the app's export feature

## Children's Privacy

FinSight Pro is not intended for use by individuals under 16 years of age.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be notified via email or in-app notification.
